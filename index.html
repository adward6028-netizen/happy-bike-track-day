The experience

MORE THAN JUST A RIDE.

HAPPY BIKE TRACK DAY introduces riders to circuit riding through preparation, practical instruction and track practice. The exact programme depends on riding level, motorcycle category and circuit.

01 / PREPARE
◈

Understand the basics

Get familiar with the motorcycle, riding position, controls and the track-day environment before practice.

02 / LEARN
⌁

Build riding technique

Learn core riding skills and understand how to approach circuit riding with clear, practical guidance.

03 / PRACTISE
◉

Experience the circuit

Put your learning into practice on a circuit suited to the selected programme and your riding experience.
